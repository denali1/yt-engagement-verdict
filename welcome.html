YT Engagement Verdict
Help make the scoring smarter

Right now scores are based on general benchmarks. If you opt in, the extension anonymously shares engagement data to help build a real dataset that makes the scoring more accurate for everyone.

What gets shared
Video ID  ·  View count  ·  Like rate  ·  Comment rate  ·  Sentiment score  ·  AI content label  ·  Verdict and score
What never gets shared
Your identity  ·  Your watch history  ·  Anything that identifies you in our database
ℹ️ Your IP address may be visible to Cloudflare and Neon as part of normal network routing. Neither is stored in our database.

You can change this at any time in the extension popup.
